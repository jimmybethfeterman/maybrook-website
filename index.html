Who We Are

Nearly three decades of sourcing and distributing food globally.

Maybrook Corp is a family-owned business founded in 1997. We source and consolidate food products from suppliers around the world, then deliver them at industrial-scale pricing to the manufacturers and distributors who need volume, not just product.

What started as a family operation now moves product between suppliers and buyers on several continents.

What We Do

Our lines of business.

Commercial representation

We act as the commercial team for producers in Asia, Europe, and the Americas, carrying their line into new markets and running the account day to day, alongside our own sales of raw materials in bulk to distributors and industrial manufacturers.

Private-label development

We partner with manufacturers to private label their products, handling the recipe, format, and packaging for retailers and brands that want their own label.

Quality, compliance & logistics

Inspection at origin, export and import documentation, and regulatory clearance handled country by country, plus booking, consolidation, and shipment tracking direct from the plant to customers in every country we serve.

Distribution in Latin America

We run our own distribution in Latin America, alongside established structures that support independent distributors with product, pricing, and the brands we represent.

What We Source

What we buy and sell.

We supply the food industry, from snack makers to bakeries, with raw commodity ingredients through finished retail grocery, along with the packaging and industrial supplies that move with them. We work directly with growers, processors, and brand owners on long-term supply agreements.

Some of our product categories

  • Nuts (almonds, macadamias, walnuts & more)
  • Dried fruit
  • Spices & edible seeds (sesame, chia & more)
  • Fats & oils (palm oil, margarine, shortening & vegetable oils)
  • Packaging & industrial supplies (plastic drums, pails, containers)
  • Condiments & sauces
  • Packaged & pantry grocery
  • Homeware (wholesale electric fans, by the container)
